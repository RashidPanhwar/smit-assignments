About Us

The story behind every delicious bite we serve

Welcome to Grill & Chill

Founded in 2018 in the heart of Karachi, Grill & Chill started as a small roadside grill with a big dream — to serve food that people actually look forward to eating.

Today we operate a full-scale restaurant with a menu spanning burgers, BBQ, pizza, fast food, desserts, and fresh drinks. Every item on our menu is made from scratch using fresh, locally sourced ingredients.

We believe great food brings people together — and that is exactly what we strive to deliver, one order at a time.

📖

Our Story

It all started with a charcoal grill and a passion for bold flavors. Our founder, Ahmed Raza, began experimenting with recipes in his backyard and quickly gained a loyal following of food lovers who kept coming back for more.

🎯

Our Mission

To serve consistently delicious, high-quality food in a welcoming environment at prices that make sense — because good food should never be a luxury.

🌟

Our Vision

To become the most loved local food brand in Pakistan by staying true to fresh ingredients, honest cooking, and genuine hospitality in everything we do.
